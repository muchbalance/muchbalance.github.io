Why MuchBalance?

What is Dogecoin (DOGE)?

Dogecoin is a peer-to-peer, open-source cryptocurrency featuring the likeness of the Shiba Inu dog. Originally created as a meme, it has grown into a widely used digital currency for tipping and payments.

Check Dogecoin Wallet Balance

MuchBalance provides a secure way to check any Dogecoin address balance instantly. Just paste the public address (starting with 'D') to view total holdings, received amounts, and transaction counts without logging in.

Live Doge Price & Stats

Stay updated with the real-time Dogecoin price in USD, current blockchain height, and network transaction volume. Our explorer fetches data directly from the blockchain for 100% accuracy.

Supported Wallets

MuchBalance supports all standard Dogecoin addresses starting with 'D'. Compatible with major wallets including Dogecoin Core, Ledger, Trezor, Trust Wallet, Exodus, and MetaMask (via bridge).
